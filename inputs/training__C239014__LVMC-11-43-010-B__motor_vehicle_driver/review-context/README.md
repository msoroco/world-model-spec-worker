# C239014 / LVMC-11-43-010-B

## Law

**LVMC-11-43-010-B — Operation of vehicles within a roundabout (B)**

> The following regulations pertain to the operation of vehicles within a roundabout: The operator of a vehicle entering a roundabout shall yield the right-of-way to vehicles already in the roundabout and wait for a gap in traffic before entering.
