# C110454 / NRS-484B-450-1c

## Law

**NRS-484B-450-1c — Stopping, standing or parking prohibited in specified places (1)(c)**

> A person shall not stop, stand or park a vehicle, except when necessary to avoid conflict with other traffic or in compliance with law or the directions of a police officer or official traffic-control device, in any of the following places: Within an intersection.
