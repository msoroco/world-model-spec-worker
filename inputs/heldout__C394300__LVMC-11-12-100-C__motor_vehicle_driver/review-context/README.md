# C394300 / LVMC-11-12-100-C

## Law

**LVMC-11-12-100-C — Two-way left-turn lanes (C)**

> A vehicle shall not be driven in a designated two-way left-turn lane except when preparing for or making a left turn. A left turn shall not be made from any other lane where a two-way left-turn lane has been designated.
