# C245490 / NRS-484B-223-1b

## Law

**NRS-484B-223-1b — Driving on highway having multiple marked lanes for traffic (1)(b)**

> If a highway has two or more clearly marked lanes for traffic traveling in one direction, vehicles must: Not be moved from that lane until the driver has given the appropriate turn signal and ascertained that such movement can be made with safety.
