# C329610 / NRS-484B-400-2b

## Law

**NRS-484B-400-2b — Required position and method of turning at intersections (2)(b)**

> If the driver of a vehicle intends to turn at an intersection and: Both intersecting highways are two-directional: After entering the intersection, the left turn must be made so as to leave the intersection to the right of the centerline of the highway being entered.
