# C102304 / NRS-484B-710

## Law

**NRS-484B-710 — Following fire apparatus prohibited**

> The driver of any motor vehicle other than an authorized emergency vehicle on official business shall not follow any fire apparatus traveling in response to a fire alarm closer than 500 feet or driving to or park such vehicle within 500 feet of fire apparatus which stopped in answer to a fire alarm.
