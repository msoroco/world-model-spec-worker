# C228508 / NRS-484B-127-3

## Law

**NRS-484B-127-3 — Following too closely (3)**

> Motor vehicles being driven upon any highway outside of a business district in a caravan or motorcade, whether or not towing other vehicles, shall be operated to allow sufficient space between each such vehicle or combination of vehicles so as to enable any other vehicle or combination of vehicles to enter and occupy such space without danger.

## Remaining requirements

The caravan ordering is front member, ego, rear member. “Front gap” is between the front member and ego; “rear gap” is between ego and the rear member. Gap values and speeds in the tables are controller requests, not measured constant separations or legal safety cutoffs. Each recording is 30 s; the common schedule includes following, a leader slowdown at 14 s, a restoration request at 19 s, and braking at 22 s.

The reused no-outsider reference is the accepted 60 m / 6 m/s recording (original job 588), linked directly in Entry front, Entry rear and Group speed. The smaller-entrant reference is the accepted Lincoln front-entry recording (original job 604), linked directly in Entrant dimensions. Reused rows refer to the same video, trace and metadata; they are not additional recordings. Entry front tests outsider presence; Entrant dimensions tests the entering vehicle asset and dimensions. Group speed contains a 6 versus 9 m/s comparison.

Coverage gap: the 30/60/90 m spacing comparison contains no entering outsider, while the front/rear entry recordings use only a 60 m gap target. There is no accepted comparison here holding the entrant, entry maneuver and speed fixed while varying spacing. These recordings therefore do not directly establish how entry safety changes with gap size. “Sufficient space” and “without danger” remain unresolved; no labels or set coverage dispositions are changed by this documentation correction.
