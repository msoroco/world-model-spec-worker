# C229398 / NRS-484B-450-1l

## Law

**NRS-484B-450-1l — Stopping, standing or parking prohibited in specified places (1)(l)**

> A person shall not stop, stand or park a vehicle, except when necessary to avoid conflict with other traffic or in compliance with law or the directions of a police officer or official traffic-control device, in any of the following places: On the highway side of any vehicle stopped or parked at the edge of or curb of a highway.
