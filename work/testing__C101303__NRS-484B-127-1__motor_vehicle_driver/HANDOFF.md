# C101303 / NRS 484B.127(1) CPU handoff

Status: **partial**. The package has two new exact cases for following a moving lead vehicle before the C101303 hill-crest lane change. The comparison changes only the initial requested body-center gap (16 m or 26 m). Both controls, vehicles, duration, 20 Hz sampling, four cameras, weather policy, and lane-change timing are fixed. These distances are engineering settings, not Nevada legal cutoffs. `case-plan.json` retains the eight previously accepted review-context case IDs as existing evidence; this package plans two new recordings and does not reuse a new recording across the two members.

The exact law remains: “The driver of a vehicle shall not follow another vehicle more closely than is reasonable and prudent, having due regard for the speed of such vehicles and the traffic upon and the condition of the highway.” Reasonableness and public-highway status remain unresolved. The frozen C101303 cell is `hill_crest` / `change_lane` / `other_vehicle`; the supplied witness specifically asks for an actual pre-change following interval.

## Files and proofs

- `runner.py` searches Native waypoints for a measured elevation crest with a connected same-direction lane, spawns ego and moving lead, drives them once, and collects `PipelineCapture` graphs/evidence, Native body and lane history, and four synchronized FFV1 views. `--check` is CARLA-free. On success it seals acquisition after encoders close and world settings/weather are restored. A failed Native attempt remains an attempt, not a successful recording.
- `finalize.py` computes source-hashed original-frame following separation, both speeds, traffic relation, weather, and observed lane transition. It writes a Native result with `worker_exchange.native_output.write_result` and does not apply a legal threshold.
- `audit.py` checks case, body, lane, frame and time joins, exact comparison isolation, the reviewed original-pixel scene-feature annotation, and full source-video decode. It cannot manufacture a human pixel review or canonical acceptance.
- `execution-manifest.json` hashes both primary constructions and one `fallback1` per case. The fallback increases the shared steering lookahead from 6 m to 9 m if original lane history or footage shows incomplete/oscillatory lane transition. It does not change the gap comparison variable. All four materialized bundles passed `runtime.py offline` with Python 3.11; their logs and hashes are in the manifest.
- `proposals/semantic-index.json` binds source-hashed relevance, law template, judgment-observable, cell-binding, and frozen two-point sweep proposals. The original stage validator passed all six stages and selected exactly `crest_follow-0000` and `crest_follow-0001`.
- `cpu-tests.log` covers lazy checking, source/time joins, negative missing-source rejection, comparison isolation, and the actual `runtime.py process-saved` missing-evidence feedback path. That fixture establishes only software feedback behavior.
- `gpu-queue.json` lists the exact ordered commands for these two cases, fallback preparation, owner release, saved processing and audit. It is scoped to C101303 and is not the batch-level queue for all 43 assignments.
