The exact law prohibits a slow speed that impedes normal and reasonable traffic movement, with separate reduced-speed exceptions for safe operation and compliance with law. `case-plan.json` maps these three branches to six exact cases and five controlled comparisons. There are no accepted recordings to reuse.
