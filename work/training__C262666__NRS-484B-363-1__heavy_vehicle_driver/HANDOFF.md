NRS 484B.363(1), with beacon definition (9), sets 15 mph for a motor vehicle in a designated school zone. Four distinct exceptions require review: school not in session; the half-hour interval after operation ends until a half hour before it resumes; an operational beacon not flashing during class hours; and a nonbeacon sign indicating the limit is off. The heavy-vehicle crosswalk witness has no accepted full-train recording. Crosswalk pixels alone cannot establish designation, calendar, civil time or sign/beacon authority. `case-plan.json` holds five controlled comparisons isolating speed and each exception, with no invented legal tolerance.
