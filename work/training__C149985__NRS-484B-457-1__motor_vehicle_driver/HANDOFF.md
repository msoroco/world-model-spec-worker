Seven selected full-train Native construction cases cover the default right-curb 16/18/20-inch parked requests, an 18-inch stopped request, conditional left-curb ordinance parking, ordinary angle parking, and DOT-maintained-highway angle parking. The authored one-way road has two adjacent curbs and same-direction driving lanes. The 18-inch value is the statute's boundary for the default wheel-to-curb position; no extra legal tolerance is asserted.

The selected manifest binds nine exact primary/fallback candidate constructions, seven 600-frame synthetic diagnostic fixtures, offline and positive/missing-source compatibility receipts, seven saved-only processing receipts, three physical outcome and lifecycle CPU fixtures per case, semantic stage proposals, and independent delivery/comparison audit self-tests. The four controlled comparisons include the 16/18/20-inch right-curb boundary, parked versus stopped, right versus left 18-inch parked, and ordinary versus DOT angle context. The new `curb_side_ordinance` audit checks fixed one-way lane and curb geometry apart from the authored notice, 18-inch request, parallel heading, parked state, longitudinal motion, frame timing and cameras; it allows only the declared curb side, lateral approach, notice and ordinance request changes. Its positive and fixed-trajectory negative CPU receipts are `native/proofs/curb_side_ordinance-comparison-positive.log` and `native/proofs/curb_side_ordinance-comparison-negative.log`. Original local ordinance authority remains unknown and the audit requires a separately sourced verified ordinance for an original left-curb comparison. All synthetic diagnostics are software and measurement checks, not original Native capture or legal compliance evidence. The engineering target tolerance used in the diagnostic finalizer only checks the authored request and is not a statutory cutoff.

The original recording must show the actual wheel outer edges, both adjacent curb surfaces, vehicle heading and motion, stopped or parked rest interval, full four-camera pixels, and physical completion. Wheel physics position units and indices, curb geometry, camera visibility, and body-to-wheel geometry require original source review. Local ordinances permitting left-curb or angle parking are administrative facts and need applicable original source documents. DOT highway designation and a separate DOT sufficient-width determination are required for the DOT angle branch. A synthetic sign or requested flag does not prove any authority. The conditional cases remain unassessed until those sources are bound and reviewed.
