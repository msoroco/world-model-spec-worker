# Prior-entry crossing capability

Two exact nonbaseline cases compare yield and no-yield responses after another vehicle enters the southern crossing first. The separate northern crossing is outside this target. The baseline is not selected. Public highway status and access are explicit synthetic assumptions; the map alone cannot establish them.

The scenario builder authors the divided road and crossing, the runner uses native VehicleControl for both actors and supplied PipelineCapture for every original tick, and four lossless FFV1 source video streams retain native frame maps. Run each exact case with its command in result.json. Later, after operator-owned capture, use `analyze.py --attempt PATH --output REPORT` to calculate full-footprint entry brackets and choose a same-setting repair or native evidence review. Saved conversion and rendering use `runtime.py process` and `runtime.py render`; neither is claimed complete here.

Unknown live measurements include actual meshing and intersection connectivity, entry ordering, controller yield behavior, participant footprints, camera visibility, channel completeness, duration, and final encoded video validity. No CARLA run or physical acceptance occurred.

The analysis checks every original trace, evidence, channel and camera frame/time join, and uses supplied worker_exchange.media.audit receipts bound to source and mapping SHA. 413(2) also computes the right-lamp pixel ROI during that same source decode. Final composed video validation remains a separate downstream check.
