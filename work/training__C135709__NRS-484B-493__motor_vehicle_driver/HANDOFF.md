This package selects two exact `park_left_side` comparison cases in the frozen `narrow_road` / `park` / `parking_restriction_device` cell: `controlled_1` requests the right curb and `controlled_2` the left curb of the same authored one-way street. Both have risk-triggered stop-location fallbacks. The `execution-manifest.json` binds their exact constructions, pinned offline checks, public positive and missing-authority negative receipts, recorded-only saved checks, physical fixtures, and independent delivery and comparison audits. The current result status is governed by the exact scoped gate.

The original Native runner records 600 frames at 20 Hz with four synchronized cameras, vehicle footprint/velocity, frame map, trace, road map, and a sealed capture. `finalize.py` reads saved acquisition only. It requires five separate source records and matching source bytes under `authority-review/`: legal one-way designation, street boundary, official device authority, notice content, and erection. Original records require `operator_reviewed_original` provenance. The runner can stage those records before sealing from `C135709_AUTHORITY_REVIEW_DIR` or `--authority-review-dir`. The standard GPU delivery invocation must set that environment variable to an existing reviewed source directory. If the original records are unavailable, saved processing should report incomplete evidence; authored OpenDRIVE signs do not establish official authority.

The selected diagnostic proof uses synthetic source-shaped review records. It measures 200 final stationary frames on the requested curb side in each case. It rejects missing or changed authority, absent notice, two-way context, changed comparison request, and changed delivery source. These CPU diagnostics do not establish actual sign pixels, official legal status, physical parking completion, a legal distinction between standing and parking, or accepted set membership. Those remain original capture and source-review tasks. No legal verdict is produced.

The frozen witness only selects `park_left_side`; no separate standing-purpose or absent-notice selected branch has source support. Absent notice and two-way context are controlled negatives. The signed source and designation roles must be independently verified before any legal applicability conclusion. Source law wording and all hashes are in `case-plan.json` and `result.json`.
