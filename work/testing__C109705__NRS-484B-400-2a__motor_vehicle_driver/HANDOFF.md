# Exact 400(2)(a) left-turn approach handoff

This package is a capability proposal for the selected two-directional branch. The native runner constructs four undivided opposing approaches, a curved left-turn connection, records body vertices and control on original frames, captures an RGB video and shared pipeline evidence, and restores the operator-owned world. The finalizer measures signed centerline distances for every saved body vertex and retains source hashes. It makes no legal cutoff or compliance decision.

The retained-speed recipe and its source-specific geometry additions are a diagnostic software test. They do not show a physical left turn. The original full-body route, CARLA map connectivity, public-way context, original camera pixels, and continued position nearest the centerline need local GPU review. The supplied C111325 training source guided the intersection layout and controller but is not evidence for this testing assignment.

First GPU capability command is in result.json. Supply an operator-owned CARLA endpoint and a CARLA-capable Python through the three environment variables. Keep the attempt in .runtime-work/.
