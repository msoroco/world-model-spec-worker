## Source and finite plan

The exact frozen law prohibits overtaking and passing a same-direction vehicle in a designated school zone or school crossing zone, subject to four distinct exceptions. Subsection (9) defines an operational speed-limit beacon as a sign device with at least two alternately flashing yellow lights. The frozen cell is `school_zone / overtake / other_vehicle` with `motor_vehicle_driver`. The frozen full-train membership has zero rows, so no accepted-evidence reuse is claimed.
