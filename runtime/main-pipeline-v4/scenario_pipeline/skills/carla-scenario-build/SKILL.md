# CARLA scenario construction

Work from the `scenario_pipeline` repository containing this skill. Read the requested cases, their cell bindings, and the active actor-specific law templates they reference. Use paths supplied by the request or existing task context; ask if a required input is missing rather than guessing. Read the parent project's `README.md` and simulator activation and launch instructions for the installed version, connection details, and available resources. Process only the requested cases and actors. Preserve case IDs, fixed facts, active law scope, and unresolved judgment calls; do not reselect laws, activate unstated context overlays, or invent legal thresholds. Keep each actor's configuration and evidence separate, and select a blueprint appropriate to that actor rather than silently substituting a different road user.

## Understand targets and preserve comparisons

Distinguish authored `world_setup`, requested `controller` targets, and unconfirmed `subject_behavior` hypotheses. A controller requests a speed profile, path, or signaling behavior; it does not establish that the subject performed it. Record actual behavior separately from the request. Judgment sweep values are fixed physical sample targets, never illustrated safe/unsafe choices. Expected labels describe recorded scenarios under declared assumptions. Deterministic probes may fix non-swept judgment conditions explicitly; actual judgment sweeps have no resolved expected label (production: inconclusive/unresolved_judgment).

Read each target law's activation, obligation, exceptions, timing, and required observations before choosing a scene mechanism. Determine the physical quantity being tested and whether the law names its cause. If it requires a curve, grade crest, or particular device, that cause must genuinely exist. Atmospheric fog cannot replace an actual curve merely because both shorten visibility. There is **no fixed mechanism for realizing a target**: only after understanding these constraints, survey available map geometry, weather parameters, props, participant placement and control, and custom authoring, then choose and explain a supported mechanism. Preserve neutral defaults for unrelated facts and participants.

Cross-check target-critical scene meanings against the realized physical world before accepting downstream evidence. A median, opening, lane direction, or control represented in the delivered trace must agree with recorded native geometry, device state, and relevant physical observations. Adapter-injected regions, map tags, and colored overlays do not establish physical realization. Retain provenance distinguishing authored regions, observed facts, and explicit synthetic assumptions; an assumption must not stand in for geometry or a control that the case requires to exist.

Within a linked sweep, pair or boundary triple, preserve all physical setup and controller implementation except the reviewed `deliberate_differences`. Compare the realized configurations as well as the input assignments: changing radius, lane width, obstruction, weather, starting position, or controller gains during calibration introduces a difference even if it serves the same target number. Do not silently change held facts to force a match. Name any necessary additional difference and flag the comparison for upstream review before execution. When the road is required to stay identical, do not make separate curves; a behavior-based visibility comparison needs a declared change such as where the subject attempts its maneuver, with visibility measured from its actual position. Speed alone does not change geometric visibility at the same viewpoint.

## Check the construction before connecting

Run cheap, static checks **before connecting to a live simulator**. Inspect available OpenDRIVE or equivalent map descriptions to confirm geometry and fixed infrastructure. Check actor suitability, units and coordinate conventions, route continuity, intended maneuver direction and destination lane, and the full vehicle footprint against the authored operating surface where these are derivable offline. Do not assume a lane lookup alone proves roadway containment or departure. Catch a wrong map, discontinuous route, or physically unsupported maneuver before spending simulation time.

Produce a reusable configuration or runner that reconstructs the authored setup and requests the prepared behavior through a lower-level controller. Avoid constant actuation masquerading as path control, teleportation masquerading as driving, or route recovery that changes the intended maneuver. Define completion and termination conditions before running; retain the distinction between a subject intending a maneuver and actually reaching or completing it. A sampled behavior need not crash. State explicitly whether its profile continues without slowing or brakes later, and why that choice probes the target law.

If no available mechanism can realize the target within a stated tolerance while preserving its required cause and held facts, report it as unconfirmed and flag it for rebuilding. Do not substitute a different cause, alter the case silently, or claim that a requested value was measured.

Check the controller’s tracking reference and segment stopping logic against the vehicle model and planned path; worker success alone does not establish maneuver acceptance.

## Declare measurements and recording

Derive mandatory capture channels from the active actor-specific templates' required observations and the case's recording description. Use the shared capture implementation to enable them automatically; do not depend on a runner author remembering an optional flag. Record the consumed requirement references and measurement definitions with the attempt.

For **every target**, state the requested value or qualitative condition, measurement method, unit, reference frame and points, relevant path or time window, and acceptance tolerance before execution. For visibility, distinguish roadway distance from straight-line ray length, identify observer and target heights, and say whether the observer is the actual ego eye, another participant, or a fixed world reference. Record the first occluder or other evidence of the required physical cause. A geometric ray cast does not by itself measure atmospheric or camera-image visibility. Static reference rays must not be presented as changing ego visibility.

Reuse reviewed measurement conventions and cite their source before introducing new requirements. A geometric visibility test does not require evidence of actual driver perception unless the task explicitly requires it. For qualitative location terms, record the maneuver’s position relative to the physical feature and evidence of its effect; a universal distance cutoff is unnecessary when this establishes the required scene context. For example, document a turn’s location relative to a crest and crest-blocked sightlines from both approaches without defining a universal distance for “near.”

For subject visibility, specify who sees whom, the incoming approaches, the body samples and visibility criterion, the aggregation across approaches, and the distinction between a cutoff estimate and a censored lower bound. Use the shared visibility implementation and retain its per-approach evidence; do not replace it with a center-only horizon or a static setup-calibration value. A geometric visibility criterion does not resolve the law's meaning of “clearly visible.”

Record task-relevant spawned static props' identities, transforms, and actual world-space bounding-box vertices through the existing scene-graph capture, including required obstruction boundaries outside the local crop or hidden from the ego. This records existing scene objects; it does not add actors. Keep geometric presence separate from visibility.

Justify tolerances by the measurement and scene resolution. A search algorithm's narrow numerical bracket is not the total physical measurement uncertainty. A result close to a statutory boundary is an approximate measurement, not proof of exact legal equality. For an unresolved qualitative target, record the constructed condition and observed behavior while leaving the legal judgment standard unresolved.

For deterministic numeric expected labels, state the event and sampling convention and compare the recorded scalar with the declared threshold. Retain timing and distance brackets in label evidence, including brackets that straddle the threshold. Describe such labels as expectations under that convention; do not claim exact continuous-time event timing or discard the uncertainty.

Save the configuration snapshot, map identity or authored map data, actor blueprints and initial transforms, weather and props, controller settings, timestep, relevant seeds, software versions, and measurement definitions. Record ego position, heading, speed, requested targets, and available applied controls over time, plus validated roadway-departure, loss-of-control, collision, and completion events. Keep one campaign/run identity and stable case IDs. Give each execution attempt an internal identity, retaining its evidence and linking repairs to earlier attempts.

Attach usable cameras **before the recorded execution**. Preserve raw footage and produce a labeled review video showing the subject and target condition. Avoid a distant, stationary overhead view in which the subject cannot be inspected; choose chase, following overhead, or a suitable close overview for the case. Keep camera pose, scale, and zoom policy comparable within linked groups. Explain every ray, reference marker, crop, planned path, and telemetry annotation, including whether it moves with the subject. An annotation is not a rendered scene object or raw sensor observation.

Review linked cases in original camera footage for the physical distinction supporting their different expected outcomes, such as a continuous divider versus a genuine opening. Make that distinction inspectable from the relevant viewpoint through the approach and maneuver, with comparable camera policies across the group. Controller success and a correctly colored diagram do not prove it. Separate physical presence, camera visibility, and nonvisual synthetic assumptions such as authority status. Clearly identified pointers or close views may explain existing evidence, but must not fabricate a scene feature or present an annotation as a physical control.

Map video frames to simulator frames and trace times. Save recorder output when supported and artifact hashes for reproducibility. Review videos and side-by-side comparisons may be generated from the saved footage without another driving run; synchronize comparisons by elapsed simulation time and label any held final frames or other timing transformations.

Preserve each case's `recording_description` exactly. Record every specified channel at its stated rate as JSONL `{time_s,value}` and authored context in referenced JSON. Include units, measurement methods, reference frames, uncertainty, raw time histories, geometry, grade, friction/weather inputs, obstruction geometry and relevant participant states. Missing evidence is explicitly unconfirmed, never filled from requested values.

For sweeps, save `realized_observables` with the frozen method/window/statistic and actual `sample_window_s`, referencing the raw stream from which each value can be recomputed. Report requested and realized values separately. Repair missing capture or failed realization at the same frozen setting; do not add sweep points, substitute the required cause, or adjust range or spacing. Videos show verified physical telemetry and evidence-backed expected scenario labels. Actual judgment sweeps have no resolved expected label (production: inconclusive/unresolved_judgment).

## Distinguish target windows from delivered evidence

A controller measurement or target-attainment window is an engineering definition; it does not restrict legal applicability or downstream labeling. Deliver the full recorded trace by default and identify its original frame/time range. Keep intended case labels, measured numeric expectations, target achievement and execution status separate. Any downstream numeric label must account for applicable violations throughout the delivered trace; missing applicability evidence remains unknown. Lean decoding verifies trace structure, not legal compliance. Judgment labels remain unresolved.

The primary review video must cover exactly the delivered Lean trace: matching first frame, last frame, frame count, and original frame/time mapping. Use `review_recordings.py` for the full review; `--previews` optionally creates separately named focused clips. Previews and comparison panels identify both their excerpt range and full trace range, and are supplementary to the primary review. Mark the engineering target-attainment window separately. Verify approach and physical passage of a posted speed sign as well as observations in its explicitly governed road portion; presence in the graph alone is insufficient. Do not trim away a violation to repair a compliant scenario. If an excerpt is explicitly requested as the delivered trace, preserve its required temporal context and reassess its expected label over exactly that evidence.

## Repair construction and reporting failures

Completing an authorized pipeline includes routine repair of missing evidence, failed physical realization, and unintended measured outcomes. Diagnose the retained evidence, distinguish physical behavior from a reporting/schema fault, and perform the repair and affected execution without stopping independent cases or asking for routine confirmation. Correct sound saved recordings through a separate audited projection, recomputing from original synchronized samples and preserving original result files. Execute a changed construction as a new internal attempt within the same run. Preserve approved targets, tolerances, activation assumptions, law scope, and fixed comparison settings. Revalidate capture, adapter output, measured expected label, and the full primary video. Use `run_selected.py` for end-to-end diagnosed repairs; `pipeline.py simulate` repairs construction/capture failures before downstream adaptation. These loops are bounded by `--max-repairs`; after exhaustion retain the unresolved case and continue unaffected work, then investigate the remaining cause. Do not replay an unchanged construction hoping for a favorable label. Ask only if a necessary repair requires a scenario/target change or a decision outside existing authorization. A mismatch between an intended numeric label and the full delivered trace is diagnostic evidence, never an accepted example of that intention.

Choose the repair from the retained evidence: a physical scene that contradicts the required trace meaning needs a changed construction and a new recorded attempt, followed by regenerated scene graphs, adapted trace, and video. If the physical scene and trace are sound and only the presentation is unclear, regenerate the review video from saved synchronized footage without another driving episode. A reporting/schema fault may instead use the audited projection described above; do not relabel nonexistent physical geometry to avoid rebuilding. Before registering the repaired pair, revalidate its physical meaning, full-trace expected label, and frame alignment. Preserve prior attempts; a differing accepted pair cannot be overwritten in place.

A native speed-sign trigger associated with a lane does not establish the entire longitudinal governed portion. When synthetic spatial applicability is explicitly authored, retain its recorded native-map station bounds and emit those specific governed regions in the delivered JSON; downstream signs must not govern the tested upstream portion. Use delivered region occupancy for numeric labels, with provenance distinguishing authored bounds from native observations. Separate commanded controller targets from observed quantities. A signal trigger at a planned route distance is not the later continuous API-indicator distance to a trajectory-derived departure annotation; preserve both with their distinct origins. Validate scalar observable references against the exact registered recording-evidence path. The integration input interval and the completed scalar report's availability interval may differ; retain and validate both. Null subject-behavior assignments request observation, not equality to null. Keep unavailable optional observations separate from failed mandatory construction targets, and require only the setup predicates actually needed by the selected law and frozen physical construction.

Use a single declared controller episode for target-attainment checks; stationary initialization and a later restart remain in the delivered trace but need not be merged into a steady approach-speed target check. Describe such reporting corrections explicitly. Prevent longitudinal integral windup during saturated acceleration and report the controller gains and limits actually executed; do not clamp recorded speed. When a corrected controller is recorded for only one member of a retained triple, declare that engineering difference separately from law variables and preserve the original target/tolerance. Do not claim identical controllers across those delivered recordings. A one-lane applicability-negative endpoint cannot perform a lane change: define an explicit straight-travel context variant, preserve its base-cell relationship, and identify the deliberate maneuver difference. If an executed construction explicitly overrides an inherited path request, publish a separate resolved definition bound to that configuration, retaining the consumed request and its hash. Do not leave a successful current variant described as an impossible original maneuver.

## Execute once and retain the evidence

Require a working live CARLA connection and compatible client/server versions. If unavailable, leave execution unconfirmed; a configuration authored without a run is not a verified scenario. Use a dedicated instance or otherwise established exclusive access when loading worlds or controlling simulation time. Track owned actors and restore changed settings; stop only a server launched for this task.

One live ego execution per constructed case attempt serves **both verification and the recorded execution**. Build, run once, measure against the targets from that same configuration and execution, and report the comparison once. Never perform a driving run to check the case and a second identical run “for real.” Identify any live setup calibration separately, retain its configuration and measurements, and do not hide an extra driving episode under that name. World facts may be measured before spawning the subject, but those measurements must belong to the exact setup used in the recorded run. Behavior targets require observed trace measurements.

For target-critical authored geometry, verify the realized setup before driving, using measurements appropriate to the mechanism and retained with that attempt. Check relevant positive and negative locations, such as the divider and its intended opening, against declared tolerances. Authored elevations and lane types can differ from generated visible or collision surfaces. When using vertical surface rays, inspect the topmost relevant surface and retain the hits; do not select a matching underlying surface while ignoring one above it. Reconcile native map data with physical measurements and original camera evidence rather than treating any one source as sufficient.

Use the declared termination rules. By default, stop at a confirmed hard physics failure rather than letting recovery produce uncontrolled circling; continuation must be an explicit case requirement. A confirmed loss of control or departure from the drivable surface raises a physical-failure flag. It **only raises a flag, never clears one**: absence of a failure is not proof of safe behavior or legal compliance, and a failure is not a legal verdict. Validate the detection method so a lane-lookup gap or wrong boundary is not mislabeled as a physical event.

Preserve every failed, interrupted, or out-of-tolerance attempt, including partial recordings, trace, configuration, errors, and measurements. Diagnose and repair it under the existing authorization, retaining the failed evidence. A changed construction creates an internal attempt linked to the previous one; it does not create another user-facing campaign folder. Report target achievement, maneuver attempt and completion, and physical failure independently: an early departure can leave a world target confirmed but the intended maneuver unattempted. Never fill missing observations with the requested values.

## Build and review

Write a `status: "draft"` execution report in the requested output directory, or its indexed `work/<category>/<cell>/<law>/` implementation workspace when none is specified. For each case and actor, include configuration and run references, chosen mechanisms and rationale, requested versus measured targets with methods and tolerances, setup confirmation, behavior attempted, maneuver completion, termination reason, physical-failure flags, unresolved judgment calls, recording references, and any rebuilding need. Retain prior attempts and make current artifact references unambiguous. Pass configurations, traces, videos, and measurement evidence onward without performing structured scene-graph extraction or Lean/legal evaluation here.

Retired C353238 pilot runners and geometry helpers are frozen under `inputs/retired-pilot`; they are historical references, not supported entrypoints. Use the current scheduler and capture tooling. Do not copy historical case IDs, road IDs, speed profiles, tolerances, or stop criteria as universal defaults.

Check configuration reconstructability, exact requested actor/case coverage, required physical causes, comparison equality outside declared differences, target measurements and tolerances, trace integrity, and frame mappings. Decode saved videos and inspect their actual framing and labels. For required map-dependent measurements, ensure incoming route extent covers the search over the entire recorded maneuver before driving. An incomplete subset of frames is not a usable replacement example: extend the map and record a new attempt, preserve the old evidence, and confirm complete required-channel coverage before acceptance. Check the actual downstream trace as well as raw capture: required observations must have usable values over their declared frame/time windows, measurement definitions must match the captured evidence, frame mappings must agree, and required obstruction boundaries must survive conversion. Successful JSON decoding or file existence alone does not establish complete evidence. Use existing capture, adapter, and acceptance checks; do not add a separate readiness stage. Run checks appropriate to changed construction or runner code; do not run the simulator again merely to validate saved recordings. Mark missing evidence or failed checks explicitly.

## Optional human review

Record optional human review as metadata; it never blocks downstream processing. Draft runs may be used provisionally with their input provenance. Unconfirmed targets remain diagnostic evidence while the agent repairs them; register accepted results after validation. Review does not resolve judgment standards or provide a compliance verdict.

## Autonomous execution and provenance

For programmatic batches, use `pipeline.py` and `docs/batch_pipeline.md`. SQLite owns job state and shared-library writes; optional campaign reports are snapshots, not a processing dependency. Semantic workers return one proposal under their stage contract and must not independently rewrite the shared library.

Human review is optional metadata, never an execution gate. Consume valid draft inputs provisionally and record exact input versions, review-status snapshots, and dependency references. Preserve an artifact's own review status separately from its sources; do not mark unreviewed evidence approved. In step 1, retain contributing annotation identities and review status on vocabulary and cells; in step 2, record the catalog facts, annotation provenance, summaries and full texts actually consumed. Later stages retain references without redoing upstream semantic checks merely to propagate labels. Judgment meanings remain unknown throughout this pipeline. Shared law-judgment-observables requirements drive fixed physical sweeps and recording; no skill may invent a judgment threshold or label an actual judgment sweep. Deterministic probes may fix unrelated conditions as explicit synthetic assumptions and report measured scenario expectations. Record the consumed requirements and frozen sweep versions. Expected scenario labels are part of delivery; interpretation of unresolved judgment standards remains outside this pipeline. Invalid evidence, missing inputs and unsupported scope block affected work; lack of approval does not.

All persistent outputs use the root declared by `pipeline_paths.py` (default `../runs/scenario_pipeline`). Do not create generated reports, traces, graphs, videos or Lean modules inside source or third-party trees.

## Expected scenario labels and complete applicability

Before accepting a deterministic case, resolve the target rule’s activation, applicable branch, exceptions, boundaries and required recorded actions. Include fixed synthetic assumptions and their provenance in the case definition and adapter context evidence; unknown required context means repair is needed, not a completed unlabeled case. Use expected_label for the outcome supported by the full delivered trace wherever the target rule applies: compliant, non_compliant, or not_applicable. Keep the intended outcome and physical execution status separate. Legacy judgment sweeps omit expected labels; new production uses inconclusive/unresolved_judgment. Do not call these labels legal labels or formalization verdicts.

For signal-distance probes, declare business/residential versus other-area context and verify continuous signal activation and actual course-change onset; a command-to-planned-station distance is not the observed distance. For stop probes, resolve the crosswalk/stop-line hierarchy and verify stationary behavior before the applicable boundary. For lane-count probes, count same-direction clearly marked lanes; one lane is not_applicable, while active cases need explicit fixed signaling and safety assumptions. Direction arrows and centerlines in review videos must come from recorded native geometry and delivered lane direction data. Posted-speed videos must identify the governed portion’s boundary and check that unrelated signal-controlled regions do not interfere. Preserve approved targets and original evidence when repairing constructions.

## Dependency consistency and recording acceptance

Prefer a configuration-driven frozen construction for compatible members of one linked group. Inspect every member's exceptions, branches and recording requirements before declaring support in `reuse_contract`; unsupported members need independent construction. Read the exact case from the declared JSON configuration. Reuse must preserve the physical mechanism and all facts outside the declared comparison differences, and every member still needs fresh recorded evidence. The group contract and CLI are documented in `docs/batch_pipeline.md`.

Freeze each attempt's source/configuration/specification hashes. A controller correction leaves completed unaffected recordings intact and active attempts on their original bundle. Update affected pending work; affected completed work requires a diagnosed changed attempt with old evidence retained. When impact is uncertain, assess all consumers. Substantive changes to a frozen case or sweep require an explicit new specification version, never reinterpretation of an existing recording. `batch_pipeline/runtime_dependencies.py` coordinates shared helper edits via `pipeline.py runtime-edit`, waiting for active users and recording affected hashes/reasons. Do not change shared helpers beneath an active job.

`pipeline.py simulate --engines PATH` uses already-running dedicated local/SSH engines on user-provided compute resources. Do not allocate or launch implicitly. Exclusivity applies to the actual server process, including hostname aliases, and persists while its runner is active. Only the coordinator touches SQLite. The existing `result.json` is atomically sealed after files close, bound to attempt, specification, configuration, implementation and artifact hashes. Recover only after independent verification; exit success or an arbitrary result file is insufficient. Retain incomplete evidence, leave uncertain engines unavailable, and continue independent engines. Never drive again merely to recover or finalize saved captures. `run_selected.py --recorded-only --actor ACTOR` performs existing adaptation/review without selecting, constructing, driving or repairing.

Resolve saved attempts through `recording_store.stored_attempt_path(store, job_id, attempt)`, not an immutable historical artifact path. Use `run_selected.py` to adapt and accept saved evidence; it maintains a run-local delivery index. Accept each full video/Core pair in its canonical `recordings/RECORDING_ID` folder, retaining all native joins, decoder checks, observations and full-video receipts. Do not export or consolidate after a case or comparison. Finalize the frozen selected law set once with `pipeline.py finalize-set --set SET_ID`; use `package-set` only for an explicitly requested portable copy, with `--zip` only when needed. Keep pending, failed, unsupported and unselected membership explicit. Judgment sweeps remain unresolved, and evidence acceptance, comparison completion and statutory coverage remain separate. See [the storage workflow](../../docs/storage.md).

## Large-scale production observation contract

For production campaigns, the supplied full Atoms.lean and ATOMS.md are the minimum observation contract. Inspect existing support and merge by semantic identity, actual actor/region identity, units and provenance; never form a naive union of names. Preserve CARLA observation → captured evidence → scene graph → Core JSON field, including required attribute accessors. A capability declaration does not establish per-frame observation. Keep unknown values unknown and report unsupported mandatory observations. Use the current PipelineCapture helper, which preserves actual participant/device states and requested body visibility without another driving episode.

Production expected verdicts are compliant, violation, not applicable, inconclusive. Actual judgment sweeps are inconclusive with unresolved_judgment; this does not resolve their standard. Supported measured reference checks establish deterministic expectations; commands and target names never establish the observed answer. Unsupported checks, uncertain activation and missing observations have separate reasons. Evidence acceptance is separate from verdict resolution; missing mandatory capture or unintended measured deterministic outcomes require repairs. Do not add an evaluator agent or run autoformalized laws.

Scratch remains the normal destination. Temporary storage is only a diagnosed filesystem fallback with registered ownership, bounded capacity, closed-file transfer/hash verification and cleanup after durable preservation. Do not delete active or untransferred evidence. Finish selected comparison/sweep groups before declaring a numeric production milestone complete. See docs/large_scale_production.md.

Use `sets/index.json` to identify active `demo-train`, `demo-test`, `held-out`, `full-train`, and `full-test` sets. Read each set's `scope.json` for precomputed cell ownership and reviewed assignments, and `selection.json` for recording membership. Full sets include their demo subset through shared recording IDs. Preserve capture-time provenance. Active membership may change only through a registered law-split migration receipt; unexplained repartition or actor substitution remains invalid. `organize-sets` is a metadata-only migration, not a per-case step. See [the active split sets](../../docs/storage.md#active-split-sets).

Use [the workspace index](../../docs/storage.md#active-implementation-workspaces) for active implementation: `work/{demo,held-out,train,test}/<cell>/<law>/`. Full sets share their demo workspace. Use `workspaces` path helpers for campaign administration and shared semantic outputs. Historical campaign directories are recovery sources; new edits belong in indexed workspaces and require fresh frozen runtime provenance before execution.

For the active `demo-overlap-v3` split, resolve law partition and scenario eligibility through `law_split` and current set scopes. Cells are shared by law-specific assignments. Preserve the demo and historical source data; do not infer partition from a campaign name or the old global cell lists. See [versioned law partitions](../../docs/storage.md#versioned-law-partitions).
